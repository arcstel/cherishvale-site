STATE OF PLAY

The claim is not a lost cause, and it is now mostly a documentation problem. The Orange County record shows the ancestral minerals (Block 3, 300 acres, William Dyson League A-10) was deeded to the family’s own trustee in 1986, was consolidated by nine family branches into C & E Land Company between 1993 and 1997, and was conveyed onward in 2021 to vehicles held by the Cormier–Edgar–Olson group. The family’s five royalty accounts on the producing lease are still on the county roll under family names. Production is current (three wells). The three documents that decide the money are the 2021 mineral deeds, the payor’s owner roll, and the 1931 deed chain for the 50-acre John Granger tract. Update: the ancestral ground is now mapped — 521 parcels of the William Dyson and James Dyson leagues. The 1931 John Granger 50-acre tract is Tract 341, and the family still holds an undivided 1/8 of it plus 1/24 of 48 acres and the Sun Oil fee lease; Cow Bayou Holdings holds the undivided interest in the other 40 acres. The producing lease the family’s royalty accounts sit on is in the same league. The 2016 Concha/Shell right of way in the family’s own folder describes all four ancestral parcels with metes and bounds and is the document that will prove the identification.

B2 Did the family ever sell C & E Land Company? Use the 10-digit SOS file numbers: 0125247600 and 0014252010 (9-digit forms return the wrong history) — and note the Texas SOS charges a minimum per record request: you do not need to pay it, because a Public Information Report gives the same names free (A2). Tim Edgar; the active affiliate CEO Investments, Inc. is run by David Olson (Bridge City). No deed out of C & E appears in the county index at all — so if the company changed hands, it was a sale of company interests, which leaves no deed. So the change of control falls in a three-year window: 1997 to December 2000 — and that is a memory question, not a records question. What happened to the company in 1998, 1999 and 2000? A sale, a new partner, a buy-out, a dispute, a death? Who was W. Tim Edgar then, and how did the family meet him? Was a lawyer or accountant involved? Those letters, if they still exist, would answer the question the state charges for.

B3 Do you know W. Tim Edgar, David Olson, Bryan Windham, or the Windham/Holland family? The 1952 deed runs Holland → Winter; a 2009 deed runs the Holland Windham estate in the William Dyson Survey.

B4 What did the 1997 conveyances actually pay? Nine family members each conveyed their own undivided share of the 34.146 acres for “Ten Dollars ($10.00) and other good and valuable consideration,” with no royalty reserved.

B6 Is anyone in the family connected to Air Products, Sample Production Co., or the Lake Ronel people? Air Products holds a 50-acre tract of the ancestral league, and the family’s own company dealt with it in 2010.

B7 Does the family still hold the 1/8 of the 50-acre tract (Tract 341) and the Sun Oil fee lease on it? The tax roll says yes — Jeffrey Dan Thomas & Tiffany Felice, 4436 Tallow Trail, Orange TX. Do you know them?
