PLANETARY EXPLORER

An offline 3D atlas of the Solar System — 20 worlds, a live orbit map for any date between 1800 and 2050, a quarter of a million place names, and street-level maps of Earth.
Coming soon to Google Play

From the whole system down to a single street

The live orbit map. Every planet where it really is, for any date from 1800 to 2050 — set a date, press play, watch the system turn. Track named comets, switch the orbit drawing style, or flip to true scale to feel how empty space really is. 244,625 place names. Earth carries a full gazetteer that reveals itself as you descend — countries, states and provinces, capitals, cities, peaks, seas, lakes, rivers. Filter by category, or search and fly to anywhere. Street-level Earth. Open any of seven metro areas as a flat, pannable map at about 1.2 metres per pixel — individual streets and buildings, from public-domain aerial imagery.

For sky watchers and astrologers

A full birth-chart engine. Enter a date, time, UTC offset and place to get the Ascendant, Midheaven and twelve house cusps in Placidus, whole sign, equal or Porphyry — plus every planet with sign, degree and retrograde state, a complete aspect list, an aspect grid and a house table. Calculated offline from our own ephemeris. Synastry. Add a second person and both charts share one bi-wheel — inner ring and outer ring — with the cross-aspects drawn between them, a full inter-aspect list and a synastry grid. Moon mode. The Moon with its real surface and true phase for any date: illumination, phase angle, moon age, distance, angular size, the next four phases, rise/transit/set for your location, and a day-by-day phase calendar for the month.

What it does

20 worlds

Mercury to Neptune, plus Pluto, Charon, the Moon, Phobos, Deimos, Ceres, Vesta, Europa, Ganymede, Titan, Enceladus and Bennu — each with its own globe, data panel and named features.

Live orbit map

A true Solar-System map with planets on their real orbits for any date from 1800 to 2050. Play the clock, jump to presets, and follow named comets including Halley, Hale-Bopp, NEOWISE and the interstellar visitors ʻOumuamua, Borisov and 3I/ATLAS.

244,625 place names

Earth's full gazetteer — countries, states, capitals, cities and physical features — revealed in tiers as you zoom. Filter by category or search any name and fly to it.

Tiled deep detail

Earth gains a high-resolution layer (~1.6 km per pixel) blended in as you approach, held in memory one tile at a time.

Street-level Earth

Seven metro areas open as flat, pannable street maps at ~1.2 m per pixel, from public-domain aerial imagery — close enough to pick out individual buildings.

Smooth, natural zoom

Zoom follows altitude, so the ground tracks your finger from whole planet down to a city. Scroll, pinch, or drag the slider; labels resolve continent → country → state → city.

Astrology mode

A complete birth-chart engine — Ascendant, Midheaven and houses in Placidus, whole sign, equal or Porphyry, with positions, retrograde states, aspects, an aspect grid and a house table.

Synastry

Compare two people on a single bi-wheel: both charts, every cross-aspect, an inter-aspect list and a synastry grid.

Moon mode

The Moon's true phase for any date — illumination, age, distance, angular size, next phases, rise/set times and a month of phases at a glance.

Fully offline

Every texture, tile and label is bundled in the app. No internet needed, no ads, no accounts, no tracking.

Real imagery

NASA / USGS spacecraft data and public-domain aerial photography, with original procedurally-generated gas-giant artwork.

Toward real

Axial tilts, polar flattening and rings for the gas giants, and adjustable orbit-path styling.
