Photo-based research notes for every file in the “mom” folder. Each of the 26 photos has its own entry below, with repeated views cross-referenced so the distinct objects are not mistaken for additional items. Values are preliminary U.S.-dollar resale estimates, not certified appraisals or insurance replacement values.

Important: A photograph cannot authenticate age, material, maker, or originality. Ranges assume the visible item is as described and in broadly similar condition; authentication may materially change them. Do not polish silver, clean prints, remove frames/backings, or repair ceramics before an antiques specialist has inspected them.
