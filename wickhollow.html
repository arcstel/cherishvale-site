Wickhollow

In development

What it is

One more run

Take a deck into the dark, chain cards into combos, and see how far the night takes you. Every run teaches the next one.

One more night

Short, self-contained evenings of play — easy to pick up, hard to put down, and always over in time for bed.

One more upgrade

Wins and losses alike feed back into the village, unlocking new comforts and new options for the runs to come.

A village that remembers

Between runs, come home to a quiet village that grows with every visit — the cozy half of the game, no timers and no pressure.

Release plan

Wickhollow will be a premium game: pay once, play forever. No ads, no accounts, no servers and no live-ops — your runs and your village live on your own device. Platforms and a release date will be announced here, along with a playable web demo.

On privacy

Wickhollow keeps everything where you play it. Runs, decks and village progress are stored locally, there are no analytics or advertising libraries, and there is nothing to sign up for — because there is no server to sign up to.
