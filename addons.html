RepTracker

World of Warcraft addon for tracking faction reputation

What is RepTracker?

RepTracker is a World of Warcraft addon that lets you track faction reputation progress across all expansions. Select an expansion and faction, view maps and mobs where reputation can be gained, check progress timers, and get tips for maximizing reputation gains.

Features

  • Select expansion (Classic through The War Within)
  • Choose from major factions per expansion
  • View maps where reputation can be gained
  • List of mobs that award reputation
  • Progress timers and availability windows
  • Tips for maximizing reputation gains
  • Slash commands: /reptracker toggle, /reptracker reset

How to use

  1. /reptracker toggle - Show/hide the addon
  2. /reptracker reset - Reset position to center
  3. Select an expansion from the dropdown
  4. Select a faction to view details
  5. See maps, mobs, and tips in the information panel
