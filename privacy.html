Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 September 2026

This policy covers the applications published by Cherishvale. It does not cover this website's host or any third-party site we link to.

The short version

Our apps have no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no trackers. We do not collect personal information and we do not sell or share it. What the apps do happens on your device.

Planetary Explorer

Planetary Explorer collects, transmits and shares no personal data. It has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no trackers and no in-app purchases. All imagery and feature data are bundled inside the app and load from your device; it does not require an internet connection and does not contact our servers or anyone else's. It requests no sensitive permissions and does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.

Purepage

Purepage is a document scanner. It collects, transmits and shares no personal data. It has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no trackers.

Documents you scan are written only to local storage on your device. They are never uploaded to us or to anyone else, and we have no ability to access them. Deleting a scan removes it from your device; no server copy exists.

Purepage requests these permissions:

PermissionWhy
CameraTo capture images of the documents you choose to scan. Images are processed on your device and saved locally.
Network accessUsed only by Google Play to process an optional one-time purchase. No document content or personal data is transmitted.

Purepage offers an optional one-time purchase. Payment is handled by Google Play. We never receive or store your payment details.

Resera

Resera removes passwords from PDF files on your computer. It collects, transmits and shares no personal data and makes no network requests of any kind. The files you open and the passwords you type stay on your device.

Purchases and Google Play

Our Android apps are distributed through Google Play. Google may process information related to your download or purchase under Google's own privacy policy. That processing is Google's, not ours; we only receive confirmation of whether a purchase was completed.

Children

Our apps collect no personal information from anyone, and therefore none from children.

This website

This website may be served by a third-party host that logs standard technical request data, such as an IP address, for its own operational purposes.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and post the revised version on this page.
