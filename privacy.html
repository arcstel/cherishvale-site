Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 September 2026

This policy covers the mobile applications published by Cherishvale ("we", "us"), currently Planetary Explorer and Purepage. It does not cover this website's host or any third-party site we link to.

The short version

Our apps have no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no trackers. We do not collect personal information and we do not sell or share it. Everything the apps do happens on your device.

Planetary Explorer

Planetary Explorer collects, transmits and shares no personal data. It has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no trackers and no in-app purchases. All imagery and feature data are bundled inside the application and load from your device; the app does not require an internet connection and never contacts our servers. The optional space-weather refresh, which is off unless you enable it, downloads public NOAA data; nothing about you is sent. The app requests camera and microphone access only for its on-device AR sky overlay and optional clip narration: camera frames and audio are processed live on your device and are never stored by the app or transmitted. It does not access your location (sky coordinates are typed in by hand), contacts, photos or files — except for clips and stills you explicitly choose to save or share.

Purepage

Purepage is a document scanner. It collects, transmits and shares no personal data. It has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no trackers.

Documents you scan are written only to local storage on your device. They are never uploaded to us or to anyone else, and we have no ability to access them. Deleting a scan removes it from your device permanently; no server copy exists.

Purepage requests the following permissions:

PermissionWhy
CameraTo capture images of the documents you choose to scan. Images are processed on your device and saved locally.
Network accessUsed only by Google Play to process and validate the optional one-time premium purchase. No document content or personal data is ever transmitted by the app.

Purepage offers an optional one-time in-app purchase ("Purepage Premium"). Payment is handled entirely by Google Play. We never receive or store your payment details.

Purchases and Google Play

Our apps are distributed through Google Play. Google may process information related to your download or purchase under Google's own privacy policy. That processing is Google's, not ours; we only receive confirmation of whether a purchase was completed.

Children

Our apps collect no personal information from anyone, and therefore none from children.

This website

This website may be served by a third-party host that logs standard technical request data (such as IP address) for its own operational purposes.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and post the revised version on this page.
