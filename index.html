PLANETARY EXPLORER

An offline 3D map of the Solar System — 20 worlds, real spacecraft imagery, IAU-named features.
Coming soon to Google Play

What it does

20 worlds

The Moon, all the planets, and major moons and small bodies — Mercury to Neptune, Pluto, Charon, Phobos, Deimos and Bennu.

Fully offline

Every texture and label is bundled in the app. No internet needed, no ads, no accounts.

Labeled features

Thousands of IAU-named craters, maria, montes, rupes and more, with search and fly-to.

Real imagery

NASA / USGS spacecraft data, with original procedurally-generated gas-giant artwork.

Toward real

Axial tilts, polar flattening and rings for the gas giants.

Designed for touch

Drag to rotate, pinch to zoom, tap to explore.
