About

Every app here runs on your device. Files you open, scan or unlock stay on your machine. There are no ads and no analytics, and the only time an app connects to the internet is when something needs it: checking for an update, or validating a purchase through the app store.

Cherishvale is an independent studio. New apps are added when they are ready, not on a schedule.
