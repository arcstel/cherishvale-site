Where these come from

Every painting credited above was made at least a century ago and is in the public domain — its copyright has expired, so it is free to copy, adapt and sell from. The versions in the game are AI-generated parodies: the compositions and characters are reworked for comedy, which is what each puzzle piece reveals as you solve.

Because the images are machine-generated, they carry no copyright of their own in the United States. They are credited here as a courtesy to the original artists and so you can check the provenance yourself.

On privacy

The Buff runs entirely in your browser. There is no account, no analytics and no ad network — nothing is sent anywhere, and your levels, stars and streaks stay on your own device.
