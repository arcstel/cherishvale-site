What it does

Liquid doses

Volume to give, from the desired dose ÷ on-hand × volume — shown with its working so you can check it.

Tablets & capsules

How many units to administer, including split and part-tablet doses.

Weight-based dosing

mg/kg doses and split daily doses, at the weight you enter.

BSA & renal

Body surface area (Mosteller) and creatinine clearance (Cockcroft-Gault).

IV rates

Pump rate in mL/hr and gravity drip rate in gtt/min, side by side.

Infusions

Infusion time, weight-based infusions (mcg/kg/min → mL/hr) and unit infusions (units/hr → mL/hr) for pressors and heparin.

Pharmacy maths

Days supply, quantity for a course, alligation to a target strength, and dilution with C1V1 = C2V2.

Reference & planning

An IV drip-rate chart for drop factors 10/15/20/60, a formula quick-reference, a date calculator and drug-name stems.

Unit conversions

lb ↔ kg, mg ↔ mcg, g ↔ mg, mL ↔ L, tbsp ↔ tsp and °F ↔ °C.

A note on safety

DoseRight is a reference tool for trained professionals and students. It does not replace clinical judgement, the medication order, the product label or your facility's policy. Always verify every calculation before administering.

On privacy

There are no ads, no accounts, no tracking and no data collection. Calculations happen on your device and are never uploaded. Network access exists solely so Google Play can validate the optional one-time purchase.
