PattyBug

A scientific calculator with a ladybug theme. Type a whole expression, watch the answer update as you go, and keep everything on your own device.
No ads No account Works offline Scientific

In development · Android

What it does

A real expression engine

Type the whole sum the way you would write it — brackets, precedence and all. Get it right the first time instead of chaining one operation at a time.

Live results

The answer updates as you type, so you can see a mistake before you commit to it.

Scientific functions

sin, cos, tan and their inverses, sinh/cosh/tanh, ln, log, log₂, square and cube roots, powers, absolute value, floor, ceiling and rounding.

Degrees or radians

One tap switches the angle mode, and the keypad shows which one is active.

Percent that behaves

250 + 15% is 287.5, 200 × 10% is 20, and a lone 50% is 0.5 — the way a pocket calculator has always worked.

Constants, factorials, Ans

π and e, factorials, and an Ans key that reuses the last result in the next expression.

History you can reuse

Every calculation is saved on the device. Tap a past result to drop it straight back into the display.

Light, dark or automatic

A warm ladybug palette that follows your phone or sticks to the setting you pick.

No ads, no account, no network

There is no sign-up, nothing plays before a result, and the app never needs a connection. It works with the radio off.

Why a ladybug?

PattyBug was built as a gift for Patty, who loves ladybugs — so the buttons are red like an elytra, the operators carry the spots, and the whole thing is named for her. Underneath is a calculator serious enough for coursework.

Available in

  • English and Spanish
  • Android 8.0 and newer
On privacy

PattyBug does the maths on your device. Expressions, results and history are stored locally, there are no analytics or advertising libraries, and there is no network permission at all — the app cannot send your data anywhere, because it has no way to.
